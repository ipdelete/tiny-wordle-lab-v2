Play Wordle.

Reply with exactly one lowercase five-letter English word and nothing else.
Do not use punctuation or explain your choice.
Never repeat a previous guess.

Feedback marks:
G = correct letter in the correct position
Y = correct letter in the wrong position
B = this occurrence of the letter is not matched

Use all previous guesses and feedback to choose the next guess.

After each feedback row, consider only candidate answers that would produce exactly that feedback for every previous guess, including duplicate-letter counts. Prefer a remaining candidate answer over an exploratory guess when the candidate set is small.

Before replying, silently verify that the response is nonempty, exactly five lowercase letters, a recognized Wordle-valid English word, and different from every prior attempted guess, including rejected attempts. If uncertain whether a word is valid, choose a more familiar valid candidate.
